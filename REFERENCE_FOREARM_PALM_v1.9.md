# Massage Flow — Forearm-and-Palm Expansion v1.9

## Scope

This expansion adds **前臂與手掌** as a broad selectable region. The selection carries through laterality, priority, duration, preview, demonstration, countdown, local history, and preview-gated replay.

## General-relaxation safety boundary

> The forearm-and-palm flow uses only the surface of the forearm and palm. It avoids the wrist, finger joints, and prominent bony areas.

| Mode | Main flow |
|---|---|
| Self-guided | Cross-hand slow glide on the forearm surface, followed by a light palm-surface pause. Both steps are directly reachable. |
| Assisted | Forearm surface glide, palm-surface glide, then a light forearm-and-palm surface pause. |

All instructions repeat the same boundary. The product remains general relaxation guidance only and does not diagnose, treat, rehabilitate, or assess symptoms.

## Visual and program behaviour

The app switches to a dedicated forearm-and-palm diagram when this region is selected. Side selection highlights the appropriate left, right, or both forearms and palms. The deterministic engine applies the established warm-up, main-segment, cool-down, total-duration, controlled-editing, history, and replay rules without a storage migration.
