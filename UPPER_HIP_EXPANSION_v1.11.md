# Massage Flow — Upper-Hip Expansion v1.11

## Scope

This expansion adds **臀髖上緣** as a broad selectable region. It supports left, right, and both sides through selection, priority, duration, preview, demonstration, countdown, local history, and preview-gated replay.

## General-relaxation safety boundary

> The upper-hip flow is limited to the surface of the upper hip and outer hip. It avoids the gluteal cleft, tailbone, groin, and centre of the pelvis.

| Mode | Approved flow |
|---|---|
| Self-guided | A short, slow palm glide over clothing at the outer upper-hip surface, followed by a direct-reach outer-hip substitute. |
| Assisted | Upper-hip surface glide, outer-hip surface glide, then a light surface pause. |

The product remains general relaxation guidance only; it does not diagnose, treat, rehabilitate, or assess symptoms.
